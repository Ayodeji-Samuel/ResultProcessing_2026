Uploading PDF Score-Sheets

  • PDF must be a digital/text-based PDF (not a scanned image).
  • The PDF should contain a table with matric numbers and scores.
  • Column headers like Matric No, CA, Exam help auto-detection.
  • For scanned PDFs, convert to CSV first before uploading.
